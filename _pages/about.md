Experienced researcher with expertise across econometrics, machine learning, and macro-finance spanning government, industry, and academia. Software engineering background in designing large-scale text and financial data pipelines, moving from classical NLP to modern LLMs and agent-driven workflows. Committed to bridging research and practice through active open-source development of verifiable data and AI infrastructure. 

PhD in Public Policy from the University of Maryland focusing on macro-finance and monetary policy using non-structured data and computational linguistics approaches. Master in public policy graduate from the University of Tokyo rigorously trained in quantitative and qualitative methods.
