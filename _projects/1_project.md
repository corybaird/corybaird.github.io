**The Monetary Policy Statement Database (MPSD)**

[https://centralbanktexts.github.io/](https://centralbanktexts.github.io/)

* **Authors:** Cory Baird, Jonathan Benchimol, Vira Vyshnevska, Iegor Vyshnevskyi, and Wook Sohn
* **Scope:** Standardized, machine-readable dataset comprising 6,693 policy statements from 51 central banks worldwide covering 1990–2024.
* **Architecture:** Reproducible pipeline combining natural language preprocessing with modern LLM tools for cross-country and multilingual textual analysis.
* **Research Focus:** Analyzing central bank communication trends, inflation dynamics, and predicting the Global Financial Cycle via aspect-based sentiment indicators.
